Long notes on the whole book: each chapter's key points, the book's own vocabulary, and what later research says. pbAnki runs your existing Book Companion skill on this book.

1It opens in TerminalThe skill may ask what you want from the book. You can skip the questions.
2It takes a whileIt reads the whole book using Opus. The Willpower Instinct took about 30 minutes. You can keep working here meanwhile.
3pbAnki notices when it's doneIt asks before copying the file beside the book on your reader, named so KOReader finds it.
